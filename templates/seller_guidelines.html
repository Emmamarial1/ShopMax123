Selling Rules

Follow these guidelines to keep your account active

Product Listings

  • Use clear, honest photos (no filters)
  • Accurate descriptions (mention condition)
  • Set fair prices
  • Update stock after each sale

Order Fulfillment

  • Confirm orders within 24 hours
  • Prepare items for pickup/delivery
  • Communicate with buyer promptly
  • Notify buyer of any delays

Communication

  • Respond to messages within 1 hour
  • Be polite and professional
  • Use ShopMax chat only
  • Don't share personal contacts

❌ Absolutely NOT Allowed

  • Illegal drugs or substances
  • Weapons or ammunition
  • Stolen goods
  • Counterfeit items
  • Alcohol or tobacco
  • Prescription medication
  • Live animals
  • Adult content

✅ Allowed Items

  • Textbooks and stationery
  • Clothing and accessories
  • Electronics (phones, laptops)
  • Dorm essentials
  • Snacks and food items
  • Sports equipment
  • Services (tutoring, repairs)

⚠️ Requires Permission

  • Perishable food (must note expiry)
  • Expensive electronics (>UGX 500k)
  • Bulk sales (20+ items)
  • Services requiring deposits

Getting Paid

How you receive money from sales

1

Buyer Places Order

You receive notification of new order

2

Prepare Items

Package items for delivery/pickup

3

Receive Payment

Buyer pays the rider in cash on delivery, rider pays you

Important: You'll receive payment from the rider after delivery. Riders collect cash from buyers and pay you directly. Keep small change ready!

✅ Seller Do's

  • Provide clear, accurate photos of items
  • Describe items honestly (mention defects)
  • Respond to buyers within 1 hour
  • Package items securely for delivery
  • Confirm delivery promptly after payment
  • Update stock after each sale
  • Be polite and professional
  • Report issues immediately to support

❌ Seller Don'ts

  • Don't use fake or stolen photos
  • Don't mislead buyers about condition
  • Don't ignore buyer messages
  • Don't delay delivery without notice
  • Don't share NIN or passwords
  • Don't list prohibited items
  • Don't harass or scam buyers
  • Don't cancel orders after confirmation

Seller FAQs

Common questions from sellers

How do I get paid after a sale?
Buyers pay the UCU boda rider in cash on delivery. The rider then pays you directly. No waiting for bank transfers!
What's the free trial offer?
New sellers get a 30-day FREE trial! List up to 4 products with zero cost. After trial, choose a paid plan to continue selling.
How much are subscription fees?
Business Plan: UGX 30,000/month (25 products). Semester Plan: UGX 100,000/4 months (100 products). Free trial for new sellers!
What if a buyer cancels?
If canceled before delivery, no issue. If already delivered, contact support for dispute resolution. Always communicate with buyers!
Can I sell on the free trial forever?
No, free trial is one-time for 30 days. After that, choose Business or Semester plan to continue selling and list more products.
What happens if I violate rules?
First violation: warning. Repeated violations: temporary suspension. Serious violations (fraud, harassment): permanent ban.
