How we protect your personal data in compliance with Ugandan law

1. Legal Framework

ShopMax operates under the following Ugandan laws and regulations:

  • Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019: Governs collection, processing, and storage of personal data [citation:1].
  • Computer Misuse Act 2011: Prohibits unauthorised access, disclosure, and electronic fraud [citation:2].
  • Constitution of Uganda 1995 (Article 27): Protects the right to privacy of communication and correspondence.
  • UCU ICT Policies: University guidelines for digital services used by students and staff [citation:4].
Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019, Section 3: Establishes seven principles of data protection including accountability, lawfulness, and security safeguards [citation:5].

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary for our services, as permitted under DPPA 2019 Section 3 (adequacy and relevance principle).

Data Type Purpose Legal Basis
UCU Email Identity verification Prior consent [DPPA s.7]
Full Name Account identification Prior consent
Phone Number Delivery & contact Prior consent
NIN (sellers only) Identity verification Prior consent + legal requirement
Delivery Address Order fulfillment Contract performance

Under DPPA Section 3, we only collect data that is adequate, relevant, and not excessive for the stated purposes [citation:5].

3. National Identification Number (NIN) Protection

Your NIN is classified as special personal data under Ugandan law and requires enhanced protection [citation:1].

NEVER share your NIN with anyone via chat or messages. Report any requests for NIN outside the verification process to ShopMax immediately.

How we protect your NIN:

  • Only collected from sellers during registration (not from buyers).
  • Stored encrypted and accessible only to authorized personnel.
  • Never shared with other users or third parties.
  • Used solely for identity verification as required by law.
DPPA Section 19: Special personal data (including NIN) requires additional safeguards and explicit consent [citation:1][citation:5].

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data only for the purposes for which it was collected, as required by DPPA Section 7 [citation:5].

  • Account creation: To verify your UCU status and create your account.
  • Order processing: To facilitate transactions and delivery.
  • Communication: To send order updates and respond to inquiries.
  • Legal compliance: To maintain records as required by Ugandan law.
  • Fraud prevention: To detect and prevent unauthorized activity (Computer Misuse Act, Section 19) [citation:2].

5. Sharing Your Information

We share your information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Between buyers and sellers: Name, phone, and delivery address shared only for order fulfillment.
  • With delivery riders: Name, phone, and location for delivery purposes.
  • When required by law: To comply with court orders or legal obligations (DPPA Section 7(2)) [citation:5].

We never sell your personal data to third parties. Unauthorised sale of personal data is a criminal offence under DPPA Section 37, punishable by up to 10 years imprisonment [citation:5].

6. Your Privacy Rights

Under the Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access: Request a copy of your personal data held by us (DPPA Section 24) [citation:5].
  • Right to correction: Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Right to deletion: Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Right to object: Stop processing for direct marketing (DPPA Section 26).
  • Right to compensation: Seek compensation for damages caused by privacy violations (DPPA Section 33) [citation:5].

To exercise these rights, visit your Profile Settings or contact our Data Protection Officer.

7. Data Security

We implement security measures required by DPPA Section 19 [citation:1][citation:5]:

  • Encryption of all personal data during transmission (SSL/TLS).
  • Access controls limiting who can view personal information.
  • Regular security audits to prevent unauthorised access.
  • Breach notification procedures in case of data compromise.
Computer Misuse Act Section 17: Unauthorised disclosure of information is a criminal offence [citation:2].

8. Data Retention

We retain your information in accordance with DPPA Section 3 (timely retention principle):

  • Account information: Until you delete your account.
  • Transaction records: Retained for 5 years for tax and legal compliance.
  • NIN data (sellers): Retained for verification purposes as required by law.

When you delete your account, we anonymize or delete your personal data, except where retention is required by Ugandan law.

9. Complaints & Enforcement

If you believe your privacy rights have been violated:

  1. First: Contact ShopMax Data Protection Officer at privacy@shopmax.com.
  2. Second: File a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO) under NITA-U [citation:1][citation:10].
  3. Third: Seek redress through the courts of law (DPPA Section 33) [citation:5].
Offences under the Computer Misuse Act: Unauthorised access (Section 11), unauthorised disclosure (Section 17), and electronic fraud (Section 19) carry penalties of up to 15 years imprisonment [citation:2][citation:6].

10. UCU Compliance

ShopMax aligns with Uganda Christian University's policies:

  • UCU ICT Acceptable Use Policy: All users must adhere to university ICT guidelines [citation:4].
  • Student Code of Conduct: Marketplace activities must comply with university regulations.
  • UCU Data Protection: The university's Privacy Policy extends to affiliated platforms [citation:4].

Violations of UCU policies may result in referral to university disciplinary bodies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in Ugandan law or our practices. We will notify you of material changes by email and by updating the "Last Updated" date.
