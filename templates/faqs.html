Account & Registration

Why do I need a UCU email to register?

ShopMax is exclusively for the UCU community. Your @students.ucu.ac.ug or @ucu.ac.ug email verifies that you're a student or staff member, ensuring a safe and trusted environment for everyone.

Can non-UCU members use ShopMax?

Currently, ShopMax is designed for the UCU community only. Local sellers near campus can contact us for manual verification and approval.

How do I reset my password?

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your UCU email, and we'll send you a password reset link. The link expires in 1 hour for security.

Can I use Google to sign in?

Yes! Click "Continue with Google" on the login page. This works with any Gmail account, including your UCU email.

Buying on ShopMax

How do I place an order?

Browse products, add items to cart, proceed to checkout, enter your delivery location (e.g., Nsibambi Hall, Faculty of Law), and confirm your order. The seller will receive your request immediately.

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, if the seller hasn't confirmed yet. Go to My Orders and click "Cancel Order". Once confirmed, contact the seller directly through the platform.

How do I know my order is confirmed?

You'll receive an email confirmation and the order status will change from "Pending" to "Confirmed". You can track all updates in your Orders page.

Selling on ShopMax

How do I become a seller?

Register with your UCU email, select "Seller" during registration, provide your business name and NIN for verification. Once approved, you can start listing products.

Are there seller fees?

Basic selling is free with a small commission on sales. Business and Premium plane, sellers pay fees according to their shop capacity and what they can afford.

How do I manage my products?

Use your Seller Dashboard to add new products, edit listings, track orders, and communicate with buyers. It's your central hub for all selling activities.

Delivery & Pickup

How does delivery work?

Buyers and sellers arrange pickup where the seller shop is or some buyers offer delivery to their residence addresses.

Can I have items delivered to my hall?

Yes! You can specify your hall of residence or any meeting point on campus during checkout. Coordinate with the seller for exact pickup/delivery time.

Is delivery free?

Delivery fees are negotiated between buyer and seller. Many sellers offer free pickup , while some may charge a small fee for delivery to your location.

Payments

What payment methods are accepted?

We currently support Cash on Delivery only. Pay when you receive your item. Mobile Money and card payments are coming soon.

Is Cash on Delivery safe?

Yes! You only pay after inspecting the item. This protects both buyers and sellers and builds trust within the UCU community.

How do refunds work?

For Cash on Delivery, refunds are handled directly between buyer and seller. Contact the seller through the platform to arrange returns or refunds.

Technical Support

The live tracking isn't working

Live tracking is currently in development. For now, coordinate pickup/delivery times directly with the seller through the platform messaging.

I'm having login issues

Clear your browser cache and cookies, or try using incognito/private mode. If problems persist, contact support with screenshots of the issue.

About the Project

What is ShopMax?

ShopMax is a final year project by IT students at Uganda Christian University. It's a marketplace designed for the UCU community to buy and sell goods easily.

Who developed ShopMax?

The team includes Emma Marial Domkoc, Nasasira Bruno, Mukisa Moreen, and Joseline Akurut, supervised by Madam Justine Mukerele from the Faculty of Science and Technology.

What technologies were used?

Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript. Backend: Flask (Python). Database: SQLite. Features include UCU email verification and Google Authentication.
