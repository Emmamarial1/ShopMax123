Frequently Asked Questions

How much is delivery inside campus?
Inside campus, riders typically charge UGX 1,000 - 2,000 depending on your exact location (e.g., far hostels may cost a bit more).
Do I pay delivery fee on ShopMax?
No! ShopMax doesn't add any delivery fees to your order. You pay the rider directly in cash when they deliver your items.
What if the rider charges too much?
Riders use standard rates based on distance. If you feel the fee is unfair, you can discuss with the rider or contact ShopMax support for assistance.
How long does delivery take?
Inside campus: 5-10 walking minutes. Nearby areas: 10-15 minutes. Mukono town: 15-25 minutes. Perishable items and foods get priority delivery within 5-10 minutes.
What if I'm outside Mukono?
For locations like Kampala, Jinja, or Entebbe, you'll negotiate the delivery fee directly with the rider. The price depends on distance and time.
Can I track my delivery?
Yes! You can track your order in real-time from the "My Orders" page. You'll see when the rider is assigned and when they're on the way.
