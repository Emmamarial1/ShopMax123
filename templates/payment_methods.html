What's Covered

  • Item not received - Order never arrives or is significantly delayed beyond the estimated delivery time
  • Item not as described - Received item is significantly different from the listing description or photos
  • Damaged items - Item arrives damaged or broken during delivery
  • Missing parts/items - Order is incomplete or missing items
  • Fake or counterfeit items - Product is not authentic as advertised

What's Not Covered

  • Buyer's remorse - Changing your mind after purchase
  • Expected wear and tear - Normal usage wear for used items
  • Minor discrepancies - Small variations in color, size (within reasonable range)
  • Seller communication issues - Delayed responses, unless order is affected
  • Items damaged after delivery - Damage caused after you receive the item

How to File a Claim

  • Step 1: Contact the seller - First, try to resolve the issue directly with the seller through the chat feature.
  • Step 2: Gather evidence - Take clear photos of the issue, save messages, and note the order number.
  • Step 3: Report the issue - Go to "My Orders", select the order, and click "Report an Issue". Or visit our Report an Issue page.
  • Step 4: Submit your claim - Provide all details and evidence. Our team will review within 24-48 hours.
  • Step 5: Resolution - We'll work with you and the seller to find a fair resolution (refund, replacement, or other compensation).

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do I have to report an issue?
You have 7 days from the date of delivery to report any issues with your order. For orders that never arrived, please report within 14 days of the estimated delivery date.
What if the seller doesn't respond?
If the seller doesn't respond within 48 hours, please escalate the issue to our support team. We'll step in to help resolve the issue and may issue a refund if appropriate.
How do refunds work with Cash on Delivery?
With Cash on Delivery, you only pay when you receive and inspect your item. If you're not satisfied, don't pay and contact the seller to arrange return. For Mobile Money payments, refunds are processed within 3-5 business days.
What evidence should I provide?
Please provide clear photos showing the issue (damaged items, wrong items, etc.), screenshots of any communication with the seller, your order number, and a detailed description of the problem.
Can I get a refund if I change my mind?
Buyer Protection does not cover buyer's remorse. However, you can contact the seller directly to see if they accept returns. Many sellers on ShopMax are students and may be willing to work with you.
Who pays for return shipping?
For items not as described or damaged, the seller typically covers return shipping. For other reasons, you may be responsible for return costs. Our team will help determine fair arrangements.
