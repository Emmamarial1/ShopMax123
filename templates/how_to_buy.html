Step by Step Guide

Everything you need to know

1

Create Your Account

Register using your UCU email (@students.ucu.ac.ug for students or @ucu.ac.ug for staff). You can also use Google sign-in for convenience.

2

Find What You Need

Browse through categories like Electronics, Fashion, Books, or Dorm Essentials. Use search to find specific items quickly.

3

Add to Cart & Checkout

Select quantity, add to cart, then proceed to checkout. Enter your delivery location and any special instructions.

4

Wait for Delivery

A ShopMax rider will be assigned to deliver your items. You can track their location in real-time on the map.

5

Pay & Enjoy

Inspect your items when they arrive, then pay the rider in cash. That's it - enjoy your purchase!

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers

How do I pay for my order?
You pay the ShopMax rider in cash when they deliver your items. No online payment needed!
How long does delivery take?
Inside campus: 5-10 minutes walking. Nearby areas: 10-15 minutes. Mukono town: 20-30 minutes.
Can I cancel my order?
Yes, if order is still "Pending". Once confirmed, contact seller through chat to cancel.
What if items are damaged?
Report within 24 hours through your order page with photos. We'll help resolve the issue.
How to track my order?
Go to "My Orders" and click on your order. You'll see live rider location on the map.
Do I pay delivery fee?
Yes, riders charge based on distance. Discuss the fee before delivery to avoid confusion.
