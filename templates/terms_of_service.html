Orders must be confirmed by sellers within 24 hours. Buyers can cancel pending orders. Disputes may be mediated by ShopMax.

6. Payments

Accepted: Cash on Delivery, MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money.

Seller fees: Basic (5%), Starter (3% + UGX 50k/month), Business (2% + UGX 120k/quarter).

All prices include 18% VAT as required by Ugandan law.

Nearby (Bugujju, Wandegeya, Mukono Mall): UGX 2,000 (30-45 min)

Mukono town: UGX 3,000-4,000 (45-60 min)

Beyond: Negotiate directly with rider after ordering.

Perishable goods delivered within 1 hour.
