About ShopMax

A Web-Based E-Commerce Marketplace for the Uganda Christian University Community

BSIT • Faculty of Engineering, Design & Technology • Dept of Computing & Technology • 2026

Project Overview

ShopMax is a final year project by BSIT students at Uganda Christian University. It creates a unified digital marketplace where the UCU community can buy and sell goods securely, replacing scattered informal platforms with one dedicated campus solution.

Problem & Solution

The Problem: UCU lacks a centralized campus marketplace, forcing students and staff to use scattered WhatsApp and Facebook groups for buying and selling.

The Solution: ShopMax provides one unified platform where the entire UCU community can buy, sell, and connect securely.
