How do I place an order?

Browse products, add to cart, checkout. You'll receive confirmation once the seller accepts your order.

How do I become a seller?

Register with your UCU email and select "Seller". Provide your business name and NIN for verification.

How does delivery work?

Our company boda riders facilitate delivery between buyers and sellers or may buyer choose pickup option, so they can arrange it with seller at agreed location or seller shop address.

What payment methods?

We currently support Cash on Delivery only. Pay when you receive your item.

Why do I need UCU email?

ShopMax is only for UCU Community. Your UCU email verifies you're part of the community.
